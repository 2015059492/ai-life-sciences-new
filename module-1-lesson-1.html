AI Integration in Life Sciences Teaching Course

Module 1: Introduction to AI

Lesson 1

Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, teachers will be able to:

  1. Explain what Artificial Intelligence (AI) is in simple terms.

  2. Recognise common examples of AI used in daily life.

  3. Understand why AI is becoming important in education and society.

1. What Is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to computer systems that are able to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. This includes things like:

  • recognising images

  • understanding speech

  • translating language

  • solving problems

  • generating ideas or text

You do not need to understand programming or algorithms to use AI. Just like teachers use calculators without knowing how they are built, you can use AI to support teaching without being a technical expert.

2. Examples of AI You Already Use

Many teachers already use AI without noticing. Here are a few everyday examples:

  • Google Maps suggesting the fastest route

  • Predictive text on WhatsApp

  • YouTube or Netflix recommending videos

  • Google Lens identifying plants or objects

  • Voice assistants like Siri or Google Assistant

  • Photo apps automatically organising pictures

These tools are built on AI systems that learn from patterns and make decisions based on data.

3. Why Is AI Important Today?

AI is a growing part of our society, workplaces, and schools. It is linked to the Fourth Industrial Revolution and is shaping the types of skills learners need for the future.

Some reasons AI is becoming important:

  • It supports problem-solving and critical thinking.

  • It makes some tasks faster and easier.

  • It helps personalise learning for different learner needs.

  • It gives teachers access to tools that save time (e.g., generating quizzes or notes).

  • It is used in careers related to Life Sciences, such as medicine, genetics, agriculture, and environmental science.

As a Life Sciences educator, understanding AI can help you connect your subject to real-world applications.

4. Why Teachers Should Care About AI

Teachers do not need to “replace” their teaching with AI. Instead, AI can be used to:

  • support learning

  • reduce workload

  • help learners engage with content

  • improve explanations

  • provide quick feedback

  • simplify complex topics

The goal is not to depend on AI, but to use it as a helpful tool—just like textbooks, videos, and models.

Reflection Question:

In your own words, explain what AI is and give one example of where you have seen or used AI in your daily life.
