AI Integration in Life Sciences Teaching Course

This self-paced course is designed to help South African Grade 10–12 Life Sciences teachers confidently integrate Artificial Intelligence (AI) into their teaching practice. You do not need advanced technical skills — the course focuses on simple, practical ways AI can support your teaching, save time, and enhance learner understanding.

Through short lessons, real classroom examples, and reflection activities, you will explore how AI can help with content creation, inquiry-based learning, data analysis, lesson planning, and differentiated instruction. The course also includes guidance on responsible and ethical use of AI in schools.

By the end of the course, you will be able to design AI-supported Life Sciences lessons, use AI tools meaningfully within CAPS requirements, and complete a final exam to earn your Professional Certificate in AI Integration for Life Sciences Teaching.

This course is flexible, mobile-friendly, and suitable for both beginners and experienced digital users. Whether you teach in a well-resourced school or a low-tech environment, the tools and strategies shared in this course will help make your teaching more engaging, efficient, and future-focused.

FAQs

Do I need computers or robots to teach Coding and Robotics?

No. Many concepts can be taught using unplugged activities, everyday materials, and low-tech approaches. This course includes options for schools with limited or no digital devices.

Is this course aligned to the CAPS Coding and Robotics curriculum?

Yes. All modules and lessons are based on the CAPS Coding and Robotics draft curriculum for Grades R–9. Activities, terminology, and skills match the CAPS strands and sub-strands.

I have never coded before. Is this course suitable for beginners?

Absolutely. The course assumes no prior experience . Lessons progress from basic concepts to simple projects, and each activity is explained step-by-step.

What tools or platforms will I learn to use in this course?

You will explore a range of tools including: Unplugged coding methods Scratch Micro:bit simulator VEXcode VR These are free and accessible.

Can I teach this content in a low-resource school?

Yes. Every module includes low-tech and no-tech alternatives , ensuring that all teachers can participate regardless of resources.
