Module 1: Introduction to AI

Lesson 2

Lesson Objectives

By the end of this lesson, teachers will be able to:

  1. Explain why AI is relevant to Life Sciences teaching.

  2. Describe how AI is used in different Life Sciences fields.

  3. Identify simple ways AI can support Life Sciences learning in your classroom.

1. AI in the Life Sciences World

AI is transforming many areas related to Life Sciences, such as:

  • Medicine: diagnosing diseases, reading X-rays, predicting outbreaks.

  • Genetics: identifying DNA patterns, supporting genetic research.

  • Agriculture: analysing soil, predicting crop diseases, improving farming methods.

  • Environmental science: tracking climate data, monitoring biodiversity.

  • Biotechnology: assisting in drug development, modelling biological processes.

These examples show learners that Life Sciences is not just a school subject — it connects directly to modern careers and innovations.

2. Why This Matters for Teachers

As a Life Sciences teacher, introducing AI-related examples can:

  • make lessons more relevant to everyday life

  • prepare learners for future STEM careers

  • help explain complex topics with simple tools

  • increase learner engagement by connecting content to real-world issues

  • support inquiry-based learning using data and simulations

You do not need advanced AI skills. Even small examples can help learners understand how science and technology work together.

3. How AI Supports Teaching in Practical Ways

AI can support Life Sciences teaching even in low-resource classrooms. For example:

  • Explaining difficult terms (e.g., “Explain DNA replication for Grade 10 level”).

  • Generating diagrams or analogies for abstract concepts.

  • Providing sample questions for revision.

  • Helping learners explore data patterns, even with simple tools.

  • Creating differentiated materials (e.g., simpler notes for struggling learners).

AI becomes a tool that saves time and gives learners more opportunities to explore content.

4. Aligning AI Use With CAPS

AI can be used to support different skills in CAPS, such as:

  • understanding biological concepts

  • analysing data

  • inquiry skills

  • communication

  • problem-solving

For example:
AI can generate data tables that learners interpret, helping meet inquiry and analysis requirements in Life Sciences.

Using AI does not change CAPS content — it simply strengthens how learners engage with it.


Reflection Question

How do you think AI could make at least one Life Sciences topic easier to teach or understand? Give an example.
